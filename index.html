Attero — Netherlands

Graduation Thesis Internship · Compost Life Cycle Assessment

2026

Completed my graduation thesis project during an internship at Attero, a Dutch waste treatment company. My research examined how the Circular Footprint Formula (CFF) affects the environmental footprint assigned to compost produced from green waste and GFT / biowaste.

  • Modelled compost environmental impacts using openLCA, ecoinvent 3.12 and Environmental Footprint 3.1.
  • Compared CFF scenarios with cut-off and explored allocation, primary-reference burdens, quality factors and process sensitivity.
  • Developed an interactive dashboard to communicate the thesis results and let users explore their own process assumptions.
